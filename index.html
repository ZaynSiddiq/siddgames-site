Dice strategy

Dice Duel

A modern take on a classic dice game. Take on a smart AI or real opponents, place your bets, and decide when to keep rolling and when to bank it.

  • Play against AI or real opponents in real-time matches
  • Quick Bet, Casual, Normal, and Champions modes, each with its own rules and betting limits
  • Totems with special powers like Double Roll, Free Reroll, and Bonus Points
  • Daily rewards, custom gameboards, and a leaderboard to climb

Idle space mining

Astro Drift

An idle space mining game for players who love watching numbers grow, with just enough action to keep things exciting. Start small. Scale infinitely.

  • Swipe between lanes to collect gems and fire lasers at asteroids and enemies
  • Upgrade your ship to earn more gems per second
  • Mix and match mod loadouts to change how you play
  • Unlock permanent upgrades through the advancement tree, and keep earning while you are away
