This Privacy Policy describes how SiddGames by Zayns LLC (“we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information in connection with your use of Astro Drift (the “App”).

1. Information We Do Not Directly Collect

We do not directly collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses through the App.

2. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process certain data including:

  • Device identifiers (such as IDFA where permitted)
  • IP address
  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • Ad interaction data

Ads may be shown as:

  • Non-personalized ads (based on contextual information only)
  • Personalized ads (if consent is granted where required)

Google’s Privacy Policy can be found here: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Consent (EEA/UK/Other Regions)

In certain regions, users may be presented with a consent form to choose how their data is used for advertising. Users may change their consent choices through device settings where applicable.

4. In-App Purchases

If the App offers in-app purchases, payments are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store payment information. We may receive confirmation that a purchase was successfully completed.

5. Analytics and Device Information

Third-party services may automatically collect limited technical information necessary to operate and improve the App, such as:

  • Crash logs
  • Performance data
  • General usage metrics

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by third-party services. However, no system is 100% secure.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access information associated with you
  • Request deletion
  • Withdraw consent
  • Opt out of personalized advertising

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
